UDHR quick help

Press F1 on any screen to open this page.

Signing in

  • Staff: choose Staff, enter your staff number and password, then the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. New staff are asked to change their password first.
  • Patients: choose Patient and enter your ID number and the 6-digit PIN set at the clinic. Ask the front desk if you don't have a PIN.
  • After 5 wrong attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. You are signed out after 12 minutes without activity.

Front desk

  1. Search the patient by ID number or file number (MRN).
  2. If they are new, register them. Explain the privacy notice and tick that they agreed. For a child, enter the parent or guardian.
  3. Click Check in. The patient joins the nurse's queue.

Nurse

  1. Open Vitals and choose the next patient.
  2. Enter the readings and save. Impossible readings are refused with a message saying which one to check.
  3. Record allergies, lab results and immunisations in the patient's record.

Doctor

  1. Open Today's patients and the patient's record.
  2. Add the diagnosis, then prescriptions. If the medicine clashes with an allergy you must change it or give a reason. Stock shows whether the pharmacy has it, with alternatives.
  3. Refer the patient to another facility or discharge them when needed.

Pharmacist

  1. Open Dispense. Allergies are shown in red at the top.
  2. Check the quantity to hand over (worked out from the dose, how often and how many days; change it if needed) and press Dispense. The stock count goes down by that amount.
  3. The visit closes when the last item is dispensed. An expired, stopped or already dispensed prescription is refused.
  4. Use Stock to add items, import a whole list or write off stock.

System administrator

  • Accounts: create staff, change roles, reset passwords or a lost authenticator app.
  • Reports: choose a date range and export to CSV. Audit trail: who opened which record and when.

Patient portal

  • See your record, lab results and warnings from your doctor.
  • Mark each medicine dose as taken or missed under Reminders.
  • Use the health assistant, symptom checker or food label scanner. In an emergency call 10177. Suicide crisis line (SADAG): 0800 567 567.
